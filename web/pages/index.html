A complete picture

Everything the options market knows, not just one number.

An expected move turns a single straddle price into a symmetric range. Implied uses every liquid strike, so you see the shape of the bet itself.

The full distribution

Seven percentile bands for every expiry out to the edge of the chart, from a 601-point density, widening with the square root of time.

The engine

Rigorous math, in one file you can read.

Quotes are cleaned, implied volatility is solved and smoothed, and the density comes from Breeden–Litzenberger, kept free of negative probability. Tested against the true distribution on thousands of chains.
