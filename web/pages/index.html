TrendSpider custom indicator · free and open source · #TSBuild26

Expected-move tools draw a bell curve. Implied draws what the options market actually prices.

It reads every liquid strike, recovers the market's probability distribution for each expiry, and paints it into the future on your chart: lopsided, fat-tailed, and sometimes split in two.

How to read it on your chart

Everything Implied paints, starting at the last candle and ending at the farthest expiry it modelled.

Dense coreThe middle 50% of outcomes (25th to 75th percentile).
Lighter bandsThe 80% and 95% ranges. A wider side means the market pays more for that tail.
Dotted lineThe median outcome at each future date.
The ForkOrange ticks: the market prices two separate outcomes. Grey: the least likely zone between them.
Summary panelTop left: the distribution at the key expiry, median and 95% range in dollars, skew in words, Fork prices.
Clear messagesNo options, the wrong timeframe or a thin chain: it says why instead of drawing something wrong.
