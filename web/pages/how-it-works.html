Implied/How it works

How it works

Follow one option chain through the pipeline, live. Every chart below is computed on this page by the same functions inside implied.js.

Market
Step 01

Clean the quotes

Mid of bid and ask; zero bids, inverted quotes and very wide spreads are dropped. Only the out-of-the-money side is kept: puts below the forward (red), calls above it (blue). The forward comes from put-call parity at the strikes nearest spot.

Step 02 + 03

Solve, then smooth

Each kept quote becomes an implied volatility (dots, with the bid-ask uncertainty as bars). A smoothing spline through them (orange) gives clean prices at every strike. The data choose how smooth: tight quotes keep their detail, noisy ones are smoothed harder, so a real two-outcome market can bend the curve but noise can't fake one.

Dots: market implied volLine: fitted smile
Step 04

Take the density

The second derivative of the call price in strike is the market's probability density (Breeden & Litzenberger, 1978): f(K) = e^(rT) · ∂²C/∂K². Shaded by the same bands the chart draws. Hover to read the market's odds of finishing below any price.

Step 05

Paint the future

Each expiry's percentiles are joined into bands that widen with the square root of time, starting at the last close. This is the real script's output for this chain.
