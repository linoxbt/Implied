Then, in TrendSpider

  1. Open the Custom Indicator editor and create a new indicator.
  2. Paste the script, replacing any template code. Click Apply, then Save.
  3. Add it to a Daily chart of a ticker with listed US options (SPY is a good first one).

Options data needs TrendSpider's OPRA options add-on, bought in your TrendSpider account. Without it the chart shows "No options data", not a cloud.
